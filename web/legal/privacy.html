Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026 · Momentra · Contact: hello@momentra.app

Who we are

Momentra is an app for Personal, Group, and Business moments (planning, memories, collaboration). This draft describes how we handle information when you use Momentra mobile apps and related services.

Information we collect

  • Account & profile: identifiers from sign-in (e.g. Firebase Auth email/phone/Apple/Google), display name, and profile fields you provide.
  • Moment content: titles, notes, tasks, expenses/metadata, invites, and media you upload (photos/files).
  • Device & notifications: device identifiers used for push (FCM tokens), platform, and notification preferences.
  • Consents: purpose-based consent grants/withdrawals (e.g. analytics, AI) stored with your account.
  • Usage / diagnostics: optional analytics and crash/diagnostic signals (e.g. Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics) when enabled and consented where required.
  • Technical logs: API request metadata and audit logs for security and abuse prevention.

How we use information

  • Provide and sync your moments across devices.
  • Authenticate you and protect accounts (including token revocation on sensitive changes).
  • Send push notifications you enable.
  • Run consented analytics and optional AI features.
  • Maintain security, rate limiting, and operational reliability.

Service providers

We use processors such as:

  • Firebase — authentication, analytics (when enabled), Cloud Messaging (FCM).
  • Supabase / Postgres — application database and related storage for app data.
  • Apple / Google — platform sign-in and store distribution services as applicable.

These providers process data under their own terms and our configuration.

Consents

You can grant or withdraw certain purposes (for example analytics or AI) in Account → Privacy & Consent. Core account operation may still require basic processing to run the service you asked for.

Account deletion (soft-delete)

Important: When you delete your account in the app, Momentra soft-deletes the account (marks it deleted / disables sign-in) and does not hard-wipe moment history, media, or related domain records. Shared group/business history may remain for other participants. We do not run a hard-wipe of user or media data as part of account deletion.

If you need a specific access, correction, or retention discussion, email hello@momentra.app.

Access and correction

You can edit profile fields in the app. For a broader access or correction request, email hello@momentra.app from the address associated with your account. We will respond as reasonably practicable.

Children

Momentra is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher age required in your region). Do not create an account if you are under the applicable age. A formal age gate may be added in a later release.

International users

Data may be processed in the regions where our providers operate. Exact hosting regions depend on Firebase/Supabase configuration.

Changes

We may update this draft. Material changes will be reflected on this page and, where appropriate, in-app.
